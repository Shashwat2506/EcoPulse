Calculation Methodology

Transportation: Uses EPA baseline factors (8.887 kg CO2 per gallon gasoline, 0.14 kg/mile public transit, 450 kg/flight). Regular vehicle maintenance factors a 4% efficiency saving.

Home Energy: Based on weighted average grid intensity (0.385 kg CO2e/kWh) with credits for renewable energy tariffs, plus heating fuels (5.3 kg/unit).

Waste: Solid municipal waste baseline scaled by bag volume, adjusted for recycling habits (up to 45% reduction) and organic composting (20% reduction).

Other: Dietary and consumer lifestyle footprint estimates based on peer-reviewed environmental life-cycle analyses.
